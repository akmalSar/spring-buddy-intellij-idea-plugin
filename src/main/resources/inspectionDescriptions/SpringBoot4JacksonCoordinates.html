Reports explicit com.fasterxml.jackson.core:jackson-databind and jackson-core dependencies in build files.

Spring Boot 4 manages Jackson 3 under the tools.jackson.core group id. Jackson 2 core artifacts are only available through the deprecated spring-boot-jackson2 module. The jackson-annotations artifact keeps its coordinates and is not reported.

Quick fix replaces the group id for databind/core artifacts; remove explicit Jackson dependencies where possible and rely on Boot's dependency management.
