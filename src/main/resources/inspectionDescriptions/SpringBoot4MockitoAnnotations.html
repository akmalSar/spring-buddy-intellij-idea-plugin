Reports @Mock, @Spy, @Captor and @InjectMocks fields in Spring tests (@SpringBootTest, @SpringJUnitConfig, @ContextConfiguration or @ExtendWith(SpringExtension.class)) without MockitoExtension or MockitoAnnotations.openMocks.

Spring Boot 4 removed MockitoTestExecutionListener, which used to initialise these fields. They now stay null and the test fails with a NullPointerException. Quick fix adds @ExtendWith(MockitoExtension.class); prefer @MockitoBean for beans in the context.
