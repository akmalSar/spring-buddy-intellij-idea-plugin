Reports Spring Data derived query methods that are likely to become ambiguous or produce surprising SQL.

Long method names with more than two conditions can hide implicit joins and make generated SQL hard to review. Methods that mix And and Or are especially risky because Spring Data parses them using fixed precedence, for example NameOrAgeAndActive means name OR (age AND active).

Prefer an explicit @Query, specification, Querydsl predicate, or custom repository method when the predicate has more than two conditions or mixed boolean operators.
