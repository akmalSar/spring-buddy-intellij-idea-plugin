Reports two or more sibling statements that all start with assertThat(sameSubject) on the textually identical subject expression.

AssertJ assertions are fluent — you can chain them on a single subject: 

assertThat(user)
    .isNotNull()
    .extracting(User::getName).isEqualTo("Alice");

Reports as a weak warning since the rewrite is stylistic and the user may prefer one assertion per line for readability.
