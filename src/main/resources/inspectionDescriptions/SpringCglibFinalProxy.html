Reports final Spring bean classes and final proxy-advised methods that cannot be subclass-proxied by CGLIB.

CGLIB creates a subclass of the bean to apply advice such as @Transactional, @Async, caching, and method-security annotations. A final class cannot be subclassed, and a final method cannot be overridden by the proxy.

Remove final from Spring beans and advised methods, or switch to an interface-based design where applicable.
