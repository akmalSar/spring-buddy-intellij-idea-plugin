Reports Hibernate annotations that were removed in Hibernate 7 (Spring Boot 4): @Where, @WhereJoinTable, @Loader, @Polymorphism, @SelectBeforeUpdate and @Proxy.

Hibernate 7 removes these deprecated annotations. Code still using them fails at startup or during metadata processing after the upgrade.

@Where(clause = "...") has a quick fix to @SQLRestriction("..."); @WhereJoinTable becomes @SQLJoinTableRestriction. The remaining annotations must be reworked manually (e.g. dynamic filters).
