Reports {@code @Retryable} annotations on methods that Spring cannot proxy.

Spring Retry creates CGLIB proxies around Spring beans, just like {@code @Transactional}. The following method modifiers block proxying:

  • {@code private} — invisible to the proxy subclass.
  • {@code static} — has no {@code this} reference to wrap.
  • {@code final} — cannot be overridden in the proxy subclass.

A {@code final} class is also a problem: the proxy cannot subclass it. In all cases the retry interceptor is silently bypassed and the method executes without any retry logic.

Fix: Make the method {@code public}, remove {@code static} or {@code final}, or remove {@code final} from the containing class. The quick-fixes offer safe automated transformations.
