Reports uses of System.out and System.err printing methods (print/println/printf) and offers a quick-fix to replace them with SLF4J logger calls (log.info/log.error).

When the enclosing class lacks a logger, the quick-fix will add Lombok's @lombok.extern.slf4j.Slf4j annotation so a log field is available.

Simple string concatenations are converted to SLF4J {} placeholders and the non-literal operands are passed as additional arguments. Calls to printf are converted to String.format(...) inside the logger call to preserve formatting semantics.
