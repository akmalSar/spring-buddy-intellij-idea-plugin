Reports write methods inside a class-level @Transactional(readOnly = true) scope when the method has @Transactional but does not explicitly override readOnly.

With propagation REQUIRED, read-only transaction settings can be inherited from the class or an outer transaction. Writes may be skipped, delayed until commit failure, or behave differently depending on the database and driver.

Explicitly declare @Transactional(readOnly = false) on write methods.
